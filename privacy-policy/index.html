Privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026.

Summary

NRI Match Handbook is a static information site. It has no accounts, no forms and no advertising, and it does not set cookies of its own.

Who is responsible

This site is published by the team behind NRI Dating. You can contact us at datingnri@gmail.com.

Information collected when you visit

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address when you request a page, for security and to keep the service running. We do not receive or use those records. GitHub's handling of this information is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers to fetch them, which shares your IP address with Google.

Information you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply and to improve the site, and we delete correspondence we no longer need.

Links to other websites

Our guides link to other websites, including government sites and NRI Dating. Their privacy practices are their own; please read their policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what information we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to complain to a data protection authority. In the UK that authority is the Information Commissioner's Office.

Changes

We will update this page if anything about how the site handles information changes.
