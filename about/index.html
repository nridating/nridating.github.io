About NRI Match Handbook

A small, practical guide to matchmaking for Indians who live outside India, and for the families who help them.

Why this handbook exists

Finding a partner while living abroad raises questions that most matchmaking advice ignores: how to involve parents who live in another country, how to judge compatibility when a match lives on another continent, how visas and careers affect a marriage, and how to stay safe when introductions happen online. This handbook brings those questions together in one place and answers them in plain English.

Who we are

NRI Match Handbook is published by the team behind NRI Dating, an online dating and matrimony service for Indians abroad. We are open about that connection. Where it genuinely helps a reader, a guide may mention NRI Dating's own features, but the advice is written to be useful whichever service, matchmaker or family route you choose.

How we write

  • Each guide answers one practical question in depth.
  • We link to official sources, such as government and consumer protection guidance, for legal, immigration and safety topics.
  • We do not publish invented statistics, testimonials or success stories.
  • We review guides when rules or services change.

What this site is not

This is an information site. We are not a matchmaking agency, we do not arrange introductions, and nothing here is legal or immigration advice. For decisions about visas, marriage registration or legal rights, check the official sources linked in each guide or speak to a qualified adviser.
