Contact us

Corrections, questions and topic suggestions are always welcome.

Email

You can reach the team by email at datingnri@gmail.com. Please include the page you are writing about if your message concerns a specific guide.

What we can help with

  • Corrections to any guide, especially where official rules have changed.
  • Suggestions for new topics.
  • Questions about how a guide applies to your situation, which we will answer in general terms.

What we cannot help with

We do not arrange introductions or matches, and we cannot give legal, immigration or financial advice. If you think you have been targeted by a scam, contact your bank and the fraud reporting service in your country; our safety guide lists where to report.

If you need urgent help

If you or someone you know is in danger or being forced into a marriage, contact the local emergency services. In the UK, the government's forced marriage guidance explains how to reach the Forced Marriage Unit.
